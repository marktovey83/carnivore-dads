# Carnivore Dads — Locked Master Map

_Single source of truth for the program. Everything below is locked unless explicitly changed._

## What Carnivore Dads is

**Carnivore Dads** is a **5-phase** program. A dad moves up when his data says so, not when a calendar says so.

**There is no fixed-length program.** Timing is a guide, never a gate. Most dads sit in a phase around a fortnight, and that is a rough steer only — it is never sold, promised, or displayed as a deadline. Nothing in the app counts down.

It is **not** a 30-day challenge, **not** a 10-week course, not a food-ban list, and **not** a "get to 10% body fat and ripped" project.

The locked promise:

- Know the factory.
- Run it on stored fat (factory reset = fat adaptation).
- Lead by example at home.
- Knowledge first. The people around you rise because you set the example, not because you preached.

Locked slogans:

- **Factory reset for the man who leads.**
- Alt: **Know the factory. Lead the family.**
- Tagline in later brand work: **Energy through knowledge** (ETK). ETK is a concept lockup, **not** a replacement for "Factory Reset" unless locked later.
- Locked line for Phase 1:

> "You can keep painting the house. It is the inside that matters — what holds it up. We are not guessing. We use your factory, and how it actually runs, to give you the knowledge to become the best version of yourself."

CD position is locked: **education and advice only. Not treatment. Not a prescription. Not diagnosis. Not deprescribing.** The GP owns medicine.

## Who it is for

- Busy family dads. Not HIIT athletes who need instant carb fuel every hour.
- Target includes **pre-diabetic dads**.
- Start with dads. Same factory teaching can later expand to every gender.
- Two tracks:
  - **Standard track** — quiet bloods, no drugs that cause hypos or ketoacidosis.
  - **Medical lane** — T2D, kidney/liver strain, treated blood pressure, or a drug list that can bite when fuel changes. Same teaching, **slower unlocks**, GP in the loop. Unlock does **not** mean the disease is gone. Unlock means labs are back and the GP is happy for the next phase.

The program starts on a **profile page**, not a meal plan.

Login fields:

## Phase 1 kit — required for EVERY dad

Not optional extras. Not "only if you are already sick."

1. Doctor visit + blood test (metabolic markers, hormones, inflammation, kidneys, liver)
2. Gym body scan
3. Smart watch with heart rate
4. Blood ketone meter
5. Baja salt
6. Fish oil
7. Magnesium glycinate
8. Gym membership — habit + engine rebuild, **not** a 3×12 program

Ketone rule in Phase 1: **three morning tests on the current diet** before the Phase 2 crash. Typical old-diet baseline is about **0.0–0.2 mmol/L**. That is the before-photo.

Gym stance: weights are not banned. Structured lifting comes later. The scan usually already shows a big engine. It needs to run on fat.

Videos start in Phase 1:

- Gut health: amino acids = structure; cholesterol = membranes and bile
- Intro macros, written in the man's job language

## The five-phase map

The "typical time" column is a **guide for the man, not a rule for the app**. The gate column is what actually moves him.

| Phase | Title | Job of the phase | Typical time (guide only) | Scoreboard / gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knowing Your Body / Factory Reset | Login, kit, bloods, scan, watch, 3-morning ketone baseline, gut + macros videos | About a fortnight | Full profile + bloods + ketone baseline. Hard gate. |
| 2 | The Dad Bod | Crash. Basic macros. Zone training starts. Alcohol sits here. Visceral fat made visible from the scan. | About a fortnight | Scheduled morning ketones + random in-app pings with photo deadline. |
| 3 | The Liver | Processing station. Intro fasting (structured, not heroic). Zones continue. | About a fortnight | Fasting locked behind Phase 2 ketone gate. Medical lane also needs GP-signed labs + written med plan. |
| 4 | Advanced Macros | Timing, amounts, when carbs return and how the system switches back. Scan as a work list. Structured workouts start. | About a fortnight | Ketones still prove default fuel. Watch proves the work. Optional second scan is data, not a beauty contest. |
| 5 | Graduation / The Room | Join the community. Not a victory photo. Factory stays open. | Ongoing | You leave with a portal, not a certificate. |

**Phase 2 → Phase 3 unlock (locked):**

- Enough time in Phase 2 for the data to mean something
- Most mornings **≥ 0.5 mmol/L across 7 days**
- At least **three clean random pings**
- One lucky reading is **not** the key

Scales stay in the cupboard. Daily/weekly weigh-ins are the wrong scoreboard. Neither is the calendar — a slow dad is not a failing dad.

## Core physiology teaching (locked metaphor)

Same physiology. Swap the job.

| Physiology | Generic factory | Police | Plumber / trades | Truck / long haul |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Insulin | Lock / key holder | Sergeant with the key | Isolation valve | Gate / docket control |
| Fat cells | Stored depot / warehouse | Evidence locker | Hot-water cylinder | Loaded trailer |
| Blood | Corridor / pipes | Corridor | Pipes | Freight line |
| Tissues | Take fuel from the blood — they do **not** crack the depot themselves | Units run on what was booked in | Taps | Depots on the run |
| Liver | Processing station | Forensics upstairs | Mixing valve / station | Processing depot |
| Glucagon | "No new intake" call | Duty inspector call | No fill call | No new load |
| Adrenaline | Extra demand | Extra demand on shift | Extra draw | Extra demand on the run |
| Ketones | Portable fuel the brain can use | Clean portable evidence/fuel | Portable heat/fuel | Clean portable fuel |

Teaching points locked:

- Body already stores fat as fuel. That is the design.
- Daily carbs are instant fuel for athletes/HIIT, not busy family dads.
- Factory reset = fat adaptation.
- Carbs later are fine. Birthday cake is not banned. The body switches back.
- Ketones form when the body **must** use fat.
- Brain and organs run well on them.
- Dementia is sometimes discussed as type 3 diabetes / brain insulin resistance — as teaching context, not as CD diagnosing anyone.
- Alcohol belongs in **Phase 2** because it stalls fat-adaptation. A Friday night prints on Monday's meter.
- Exogenous ketone drinks, one long fast + a steak, wrecked sleep, illness, very low calories, or SGLT2 meds can fake or scramble a reading. Ketones mean fat is on duty **right now**. They do not mean Phase 3 forever.

## The Room (after graduation)

The five phases are the factory reset. **The Room is what they keep.**

Same login. Same profile. Not a certificate.

Four screens locked in `CD_The_Room_Portal.pdf`:

1. **Recipes** — carnivore meals in occupation language. Family table, not restaurant plating. Gated to members.
2. **Member shop** — Baja salt, fish oil, magnesium glycinate, ketone strips, merch. Member price lower than public cart. Discount lives on the account, not a Facebook coupon.
3. **History** — phase images, ketone trend, kit dates, shop orders. Stickiest screen. Drives restock.
4. **Keep getting** — new recipes, short factory reminders, seasonal merch. After-care bay, not a dump of week-1 videos.

Who gets in:

- Phase 5 graduation unlocks the Room
- Medical-lane men unlock when their GP path allows the same gates
- Trial users see shop at full price and a locked recipe shelf
